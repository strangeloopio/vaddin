The Happy Announcement

Families Overjoyed as Couple Sets the Date

It is with great joy — and very little surprise to anyone who has ever seen them together — that the families of Vrudhi and Achal announce the wedding of the bride and groom, to be solemnised on Thursday, the 25th of February, 2027, at Club O7, Ahmedabad.

Festivities open a day earlier, on Wednesday the 24th, when both families gather for the pre-wedding celebrations: an evening expected to feature music, dancing, and at least one aunty or uncle who insists they “knew it all.”

Reporters pressed the couple for comment. The bride smiled. The groom grinned. Sources close to both confirm that a lifetime subscription has now been mutually agreed upon.

Venue Desk

Club O7 Braces for Two Days of Festivities

Preparations are well underway across the city. Florists report record orders, tailors are working by lamplight, and the dance floor at Club O7 is said to be undergoing structural reinforcement in anticipation of the baraat.

Guests are advised to rest their feet, press their finest attire, and keep both evenings entirely free of other engagements. Officials confirm that no excuses will be entertained.
